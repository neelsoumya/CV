\section*{Research Profile}
AI researcher working across \textbf{explainable and human-centred AI, machine learning, healthcare AI, abstraction and reasoning, and computational approaches to intelligence}. \section*{Selected Research Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Explainable and trustworthy AI:} human-interpretable machine learning and patient/public involvement frameworks for healthcare AI.
\item \textbf{Healthcare AI:} machine learning on electronic healthcare records for patient stratification and mortality prediction, including work published in \textit{NPJ Schizophrenia}.
\item \textbf{Abstraction and reasoning:} research on neural networks and AI systems for abstraction and reasoning, including work presented at the NeurIPS Multimodal Algorithmic Reasoning Workshop.
\item \textbf{Computational biology and complex systems:} mathematical and computational modelling of immune systems, infectious diseases, cell signalling, and socio-economic systems.
\end{itemize}
