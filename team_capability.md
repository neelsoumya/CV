## Team and capability

## Module 1 Contributions to the generation of new ideas, tools, methodologies or knowledge

**Dr Banerjee** builds AI methods that people can inspect, question and trust, which is the capability the AI financial coach needs most. 
He developed a class-contrastive, human-interpretable machine learning approach that predicts mortality in people with severe mental illness from electronic health records and offers explanations a human can interpret (NPJ Schizophrenia, 2021). He also created a framework, tools and case studies for patient and public involvement in AI development (Patterns journal, 2022), a participatory route to AI model building. 

## Module 2 The development of others and maintenance of effective working relationships

**Dr Banerjee** has supervised 17 MPhil students and 3 summer interns and co-supervised one PhD student, and has co-authored work with early-career collaborators at venues including *Scientific Reports*, and AI conferences such as NeurIPS. He is a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy. He has taught machine learning at the University of Cambridge and has developed openly available courses and co-designed teaching resources with educators so that material is accessible beyond the classroom. His working relationships are long-standing and interdisciplinary: he has published repeatedly with academics from different disciplines.

## Module 3 Contributions to the wider research and innovation community

**Dr Banerjee** contributes to the research community through programme committees, editorial work and peer review. He serves on the programme committee of AI workshops such as the *International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR) workshop, From Human Cognition to AI Reasoning: Models, Methods, and Applications*. He has also sat on recruitment panels for PhD, MPhil and MSc students. His invited talks include a keynote at the Physics of Self-Organization in Complex Systems satellite meeting of the Conference on Complex Systems (Exeter, 2024), a talk at UK-India Cooperation Towards a Fair AI Horizon (2024) and a talk on responsible AI and patient involvement at Nokia Bell Labs (2025). He has provided expertise to the Templeton Foundation (2025) and the American University of Dubai (2026).

## Module 4 Contributions to broader research/innovation-users and audiences and towards wider societal benefit

**Dr Banerjee** orients his research towards non-specialist audiences and communities that mainstream AI under-serves. He co-developed a framework and tools for involving patients and the public in AI development, published with case studies ([Patterns journal, 2022](https://www.cell.com/patterns/fulltext/S2666-3899%2822%2900098-8)), and has argued for involving patients to build trustworthy systems (AI and Society journal, 2023). This is methodologically close to the TfD co-creation approach proposed here. He also wrote in [*The Lancet Rheumatology*](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanrhe/article/PIIS2665-9913%2820%2930162-4/fulltext) on balancing the needs of low and middle income countries in hydroxychloroquine supply during the COVID-19 pandemic. His open-source software includes R packages for privacy-preserving analysis of clinical data across cohorts and MATLAB code ranked within the top 250 worldwide among MATLAB Central open-source contributors by download counts.
